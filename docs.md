# All About Cats

Cats are small, furry animals that people keep as pets all over the world.

## Basic Facts
- Cats sleep for about 12 to 16 hours a day
- A group of kittens is called a kindle
- Cats communicate with meows, purrs and body language
- They are known for being curious and independent

## Popular Cat Breeds
1. Persian
2. Siamese
3. Maine Coon
4. British Shorthair

## Cat Care Tips
- Provide fresh water and a balanced diet every day
- Give them a clean litter box
- Take them to the vet for regular checkups
- Play with them to keep them active and happy
